Before Contacting Support

Checklist

  • Check your inbox and spam folder for the delivery email.
  • Open your order page and complete the key reveal verification step.
  • Confirm the payment status on the checkout return page.
  • Make sure the platform and region match the purchased product.
  • For guest orders, use the same email address used at checkout.
